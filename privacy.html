Homeprint (ہوم پرنٹ) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026
App: Homeprint: Home Manual & Guide (pk.arslan.homeprint)
Developer: Global Hosting Service, Islamabad, Pakistan
Contact: me@arslan.pk

1. What Homeprint is

Homeprint is a home manual app. You write down information about your home — Wi-Fi details, emergency points, contacts, how-to guides with photos, staff tasks, care routines, house rules and reminders — and you can share selected parts as a PDF guide, a message or an emergency card with people you choose.

2. Your data stays on your phone

Everything you enter in Homeprint, including photos, is stored only on your own device. Homeprint has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own; we do not receive, see or store your home information. Data leaves your phone only when you share it (for example a PDF guide sent through WhatsApp or email, or a backup file you save) — and then only to the app and people you choose.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone — only when you tap the microphone button to dictate text. Speech is converted to text by your phone's speech-recognition service; Homeprint does not record or keep audio.
  • Camera / photos — only when you choose to add a photo to a card. Photos are kept inside the app on your phone.
  • Notifications — to remind you about bills, warranties and servicing dates you added. Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself.

Read-aloud uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine.

4. Advertising

The free app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and app interaction data, as described in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) you are asked for consent, and you can reset or limit ad personalisation in your Android settings. Your home information is never shared with advertisers.

5. Children

Homeprint is a household organisation tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete any card, or a whole home, inside the app. Uninstalling Homeprint or clearing its data in Android settings deletes everything from your phone. Because we do not hold any of your data on a server, there is nothing else to delete; if you have questions, email me@arslan.pk.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: me@arslan.pk
